# Hydrogen Water Datasets

This folder contains Open Biohacking Data Project reference files related to hydrogen water, dissolved molecular hydrogen, PPB and PPM concentration references, hydrogen water bottle education, and hydration-related wellness technology.

## Scope

The hydrogen water dataset category may include:

- Hydrogen water reference data
- PPB and PPM concentration notes
- Hydrogen water versus alkaline water comparisons
- Bottle-use education references
- Cleaning and maintenance notes
- Beginner hydration routine references
