# Negative Ion Datasets

This folder contains Open Biohacking Data Project reference files related to negative ion wellness devices, negative ion pendant education, air ionization references, and beginner safety considerations.

## Scope

The negative ion dataset category may include:

- Negative ion safety references
- Negative ion pendant education
- Air ionization notes
- Device-use considerations
- Beginner safety references
- General wellness technology education

This folder is for educational and informational purposes only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, cure, prevention, or a substitute for professional healthcare guidance.
